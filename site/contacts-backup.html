Questions

Before you write.

What happens after I send this form?

It creates a lead in our CRM and a member of the team replies within [X] working hours with either a scoping question or a quote. Nothing is added to a marketing list.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing details?

Yes, and we prefer it. Send your standard NDA with the enquiry, or ask for ours. No client data needs to be shared to get an indicative quote — the transaction type, jurisdictions and years are enough.

Do you take small, one-off engagements?

Yes. A single benchmarking study is a normal first engagement and is how most long-term relationships here started.
