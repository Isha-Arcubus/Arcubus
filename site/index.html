Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does Arcubus Advisors do?

Arcubus Advisors is a transfer pricing boutique in Pune, India. We prepare TP benchmarking studies (TNMM and CUP margins, financial transactions under OECD Chapter X, intangibles and royalty rates), white-label TP documentation (Local File, Master File, CbCR), TP advisory and managed TP services. Our clients are global consulting and CPA firms who need a delivery team behind their engagements, and in-house tax teams who need capacity and a defensible file.

Will the report carry our firm’s name?

Yes. White-label delivery is the default for consulting firms: we work on your template, in your house style, with your file naming and your review conventions, and you issue the deliverable to your client. Arcubus is not named in the report and does not contact your client.

Which databases do you search?

We run searches on [list the databases your firm subscribes to — e.g. commercial company financial databases for regional and global comparables, and loan/bond pricing sources for Chapter X work]. Where a client prefers a specific database or has its own subscription, we work inside that.

How long does a benchmarking study take?

We confirm scope and acceptance within 1 working day of receiving the benchmarking request. Preliminary results follow [X] working days after you approve the search strategy, and the final report [X] working days after your comments. Rush timelines are agreed in writing before we start rather than promised generically.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We sign your NDA before any data changes hands, work only from the files you share, restrict access to the team on your engagement, and can work inside your own systems and document management where your firm requires it.

How do we start working with you?

Send a benchmarking request or book a call. The first conversation is free and without obligation — we scope the transaction, confirm whether we are the right fit, and give you a fixed quote and timeline before any work begins.
