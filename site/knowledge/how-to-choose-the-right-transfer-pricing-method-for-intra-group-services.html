Transfer Pricing Methods

How to choose the right transfer pricing method for INTRA-GROUP services.

Selecting the right transfer pricing method for INTRA-GROUP services is critical to achieving an arm’s-length outcome.

Arcubus Advisors07 September 20266 min read

Key takeaways

  • The most appropriate method depends on the nature of the service, the availability of comparable data, and the degree of value creation.
  • CUP and cost-plus are the most commonly used methods for routine INTRA-GROUP services; TNMM is preferred when comparables are limited.
  • Low-value-adding services can often use a simplified cost-plus approach with a 5% mark-up under the OECD Guidelines.
  • Documentation must clearly explain why the chosen method was selected and why alternatives were rejected.
  • Using the wrong method is one of the most common reasons for transfer pricing adjustments during tax audits.
