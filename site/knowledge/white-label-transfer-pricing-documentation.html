White-label delivery

What white-label TP documentation actually involves.

How white-label transfer pricing support works in practice: what the outsourcing firm does, what stays with your firm, how confidentiality is handled, and what to test on the first file.

Arcubus Advisors30 April 20266 min read

Key takeaways

  • White-label means the analysis is prepared by the support firm and issued by your firm, on your template, with the support firm unnamed and never in contact with your client.
  • What stays with your firm: the client relationship, the professional opinion, the review, and the filing.
  • The test of a white-label provider is the audit trail and the writing quality, not the price — a file you have to rebuild is not cheaper.
  • Start with one deliverable, agreed in writing on scope, format, turnaround and non-solicitation.

"White-label" is used loosely. In transfer pricing it has a precise meaning worth stating plainly: a support firm prepares the analysis and the draft, your firm reviews and issues it under its own name, and the support firm is neither named nor in contact with your client.

What "on your template" really requires

Matching a house style is more than a logo swap. A provider that has absorbed your format will match your section order and numbering, your defined terms, your table conventions and units, your disclaimer and limitation language, your file naming and version conventions, and the level of assertiveness your reviewers expect in the narrative. Ask for a marked-up prior file at onboarding; it removes two rounds of comments from every subsequent engagement.

The audit trail is the product

A finished report is easy to produce. What makes a white-label file usable is everything behind it: the approved strategy note, the search log with reconciled counts, the rejection matrix with evidence, the source extracts for each accepted comparable, and the range workings. If those do not arrive with the draft, your firm carries a defence risk it cannot see.

Commercial terms worth fixing in writing

  • Non-solicitation — the support firm does not approach your client, during the engagement or after it.
  • Confidentiality and access — who on the support side sees the data, and where it is stored.
  • Turnaround per deliverable — with the trigger point named (for us, acceptance within 1 working day of the request; results a fixed number of working days from strategy approval).
  • Comment rounds included — and what triggers a re-quote.
  • Ownership of working papers — and the retention period.

Why firms do it

Two reasons dominate. Smaller and mid-size firms want to offer transfer pricing without building a practice and hiring specialists for uneven volume. Larger firms want elastic capacity through filing season without permanent headcount. In both cases the economics only work if the deliverable arrives review-ready — a file your team has to rebuild is not cheaper, it is more expensive and later.

Questions

On this topic.

Is white-label outsourcing permitted for our clients?

The professional and independence rules that apply to you do not change because part of the work is prepared by a support firm — you remain responsible for the deliverable, and your engagement letter and client consent requirements govern whether and how you disclose the arrangement. Check your own institute’s rules and your engagement terms; we work to whatever disclosure position you take.

How do you keep our client’s identity confidential internally?

Where you prefer it, we work on anonymised entity names and a client code, with the identifying financials shared only where the analysis needs them. Access is limited to the named engagement team.

What does a first engagement usually look like?

One benchmarking study or one Local File, with scope, format, turnaround and fee fixed in writing. It gives both sides a real sample: you see the trail and the drafting, we see your review standard.

What happens if your draft is not up to our standard?

It gets re-worked against your comments at no extra fee within the agreed scope. If the gap is structural — a house style we have not absorbed yet — we ask for a marked-up example and update our internal checklist so the second file lands closer.
