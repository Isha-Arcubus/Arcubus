Process

A standard process behind every deliverable.

The ten steps below are how a TNMM benchmarking study runs at Arcubus. Documentation engagements follow the same shape: structured intake, your approval, internal review, then delivery.

In short

A TNMM benchmarking study at Arcubus runs in ten steps: request form, scope confirmation within 1 working day, search strategy proposal, your approval, database search and quantitative screens, qualitative review with a rejection matrix, internal checklist review, preliminary results, your comments, and a final report on your template with the search log annexed.

Two gates matter: nothing is searched before you approve the strategy, and nothing is issued before a second reviewer has re-performed the screens.

TNMM benchmarking — step by step

Checked internally before it ever reaches you.

1 Client

You send the benchmarking request

A short structured form captures the tested party, the transaction, the years, the jurisdictions, the PLI you expect and any prior-year comparables set.

2 Arcubus

We confirm scope and acceptance

We check the request for gaps, flag anything that changes the approach, and confirm price and timeline in writing before starting.

⏱ Within 1 working day of receiving the request
3 Arcubus

We propose the search strategy

Database, region, activity codes, independence and turnover screens, loss-making treatment, year set and PLI — written up as a strategy note.

4 Client

You approve the strategy

Nothing is searched until you have signed off on the methodology. Changes at this stage cost nothing; changes after the search cost time.

5 Arcubus

We run the search and quantitative screens

The accepted strategy is executed and every screen is logged with the count of companies removed at each step.

6 Arcubus

We review each candidate qualitatively

Business descriptions, websites and annual reports are read company by company. Each rejection is recorded with a reason in the rejection matrix.

7 Arcubus

Internal review against the checklist

A second reviewer re-performs the screens, checks the arithmetic of the range and confirms the file matches the approved strategy.

8 Arcubus

Preliminary results to you

The range, the accepted set and the rejection matrix, with the workings, so you can react before the report is written.

Questions

Process questions.

Why do you insist on approving the search strategy before searching?

Because the strategy is what a reviewer or a tax authority challenges — not the arithmetic. Agreeing the databases, screens and PLI up front means the study defends a position your firm has already accepted, and it prevents an expensive re-run after results are in.

What is a rejection matrix and why does it matter?

A rejection matrix lists every company thrown out of the search and the specific reason for each exclusion: wrong activity, controlled transactions, insufficient data, persistent losses, related-party revenue above the threshold. It is the single most challenged part of a benchmarking file, so we hand it over as a standalone annexure.

How many rounds of comments are included?

Two rounds on the preliminary results and one on the final draft are included in the standard quote. Beyond that, or where the transaction characterisation changes, we re-quote before doing the work.

What happens if the search produces an unusable set?

We tell you early rather than at delivery. Options are documented and priced: widen the geography, relax a screen with reasoning, change the PLI, move to a different method, or use multiple-year data. You choose which route the file takes.
