Services

Transfer pricing work, delivered to your standard.

Four service lines: benchmarking, white-label documentation, advisory and managed TP services. Each one runs on the same documented process and can be delivered under your brand.

Arcubus delivers four transfer pricing service lines to consulting firms and in-house tax teams:

  • TP benchmarking — TNMM and CUP margin studies, OECD Chapter X financial transactions, royalty and intangibles / DEMPE analyses.
  • White-label documentation — Local File, Master File and CbCR support issued under your firm’s brand.

01 — TP Benchmarking

Benchmarking that survives a reviewer.

A benchmarking study is only as good as its search documentation. We hand over the accept / reject reasoning, the screening trail and the workings — not just a margin range.

What a study includes

  • Tested party and transaction characterisation, agreed with you before searching
  • Database selection, search strategy and screening criteria for your approval
  • Quantitative screens: activity, independence, turnover, loss-making and data availability
  • Qualitative review of each candidate with a documented rejection matrix
  • Arm’s length range: quartiles, weighted averages and multiple-year data as required
  • Sensitivity workings and working capital or other economic adjustments where relevant
  • Final report in your template, with the search log attached as an annexure

Transaction types we cover

Services & distribution

Routine and value-added services, contract and full-fledged manufacturing, limited-risk and full distribution, commission and agency arrangements — TNMM with PLI selection reasoned in the file.

Financial transactions

Intra-group loans, guarantees, cash pooling and receivables under OECD Chapter X: borrower credit rating, term and currency matching, and arm’s length spread analysis.

Intangibles & royalties

Royalty rate benchmarking from agreement databases, brand and technology licence comparisons, and DEMPE functional mapping to support the return to each entity.

Management charges

Cost pool composition, allocation key selection, benefit tests and mark-up benchmarking for shared and management services.

03 — TP Advisory

Advice on the position, not just the paperwork.

Where we are typically brought in

  • Designing or resetting a group TP policy after a restructuring, acquisition or new entity
  • Method and PLI selection where more than one approach is arguable
  • Drafting or reviewing intra-group agreements so they match what the entities actually do
  • Preparing responses to notices, audits and assessment questions, with supporting workings
  • Second opinions on a position already taken, before it is tested
  • Health checks across an existing documentation set to find inconsistencies before a reviewer does

How advisory work is scoped

Advisory is quoted either as a fixed-scope note with a defined question and deliverable, or as an hourly block for open-ended support during an audit. We say which is appropriate at the scoping call.

What we will not do

We do not sign off on your client’s position, appear before authorities in your place, or take over your client relationship. On white-label engagements the opinion stays with your firm.

Questions

Service questions we are asked most.

Can you benchmark a transaction where local comparables are thin?

Yes. Where the local market gives too few comparables we widen to a regional or pan-European / pan-Asian set and document the reason for the widening, the geographic adjustment considered, and why the final set is still reliable. The rejection reasoning is what defends the study, not the size of the set.

Do you prepare the whole Local File or only the benchmarking chapter?

Either. Many consulting firms send us only the economic analysis; others hand over the full file including functional analysis, industry overview and intra-group transaction schedules. Scope is fixed in writing before we begin.

Can you work on intra-group loans, guarantees and cash pooling?

Yes — financial transactions are a distinct workstream under OECD Chapter X: credit rating of the borrower, term and currency matching, arm’s length interest rate ranges, guarantee fee analysis and cash pool reward allocation.

Do you sign the report or take a position on it?

No. On white-label engagements the professional opinion and the client relationship stay with your firm. We deliver the analysis, the workings and the audit trail; you review and issue.
