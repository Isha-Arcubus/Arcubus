Who we serve

Two audiences, two ways of working.

Consulting and CPA firms use us as an invisible delivery team. In-house tax teams use us as capacity with a documented trail. The process is the same; the packaging is not.

In short

Arcubus serves two kinds of client:

  • Global consulting, tax and CPA firms — white-label benchmarking and documentation delivered under your brand, on your templates, with no contact with your client.
  • In-house tax teams at corporates — additional TP capacity for benchmarking, Local Files, CbCR and annual refreshes, with the audit trail handed over.

01 — Consulting & CPA firms

The work is ours; the letterhead is yours.

You win the engagement and own the relationship. We run the analysis behind it, to your format and your review standard, and stay out of sight.

What you get

  • Deliverables in your template, your styles and your file naming — ready for your review
  • A named point of contact and a fixed turnaround per deliverable
  • Search logs and workings handed over, so your file is defensible without us
  • Capacity that scales through filing season without adding permanent headcount
  • A cost base that lets you price TP work competitively against larger firms
  • Strict non-solicitation: your client stays yours

How an engagement runs

You send the request form. We confirm scope within 1 working day, propose the search strategy for your approval, deliver preliminary results, then issue the final draft on your template. Your reviewer signs it; we are never named.

Starting small

Most firms start with a single benchmarking study or one Local File. If the trail, the writing and the turnaround hold up, volume follows. We would rather earn it that way.

Questions

What firms and tax teams ask first.

Do you work directly with our clients?

No, unless you ask us to. On white-label engagements all communication runs through your engagement team. We do not contact your client, appear in the deliverable, or approach them for our own work — during the engagement or after it.

We are a small CPA firm without a TP practice. Can we still offer TP?

That is the most common reason firms come to us. You keep the client relationship and the review; we supply the benchmarking, drafting and workings behind it. Many firms start with one file to test the process before routing volume to us.

We are an in-house tax team. What do you take off our plate?

Typically the benchmarking searches, the first drafts of the Local Files, the CbCR table population and the annual refresh of comparables — the recurring work that consumes a small team’s year. Your team keeps the judgement calls and the filings.

Can you work inside our systems?

Yes. Under managed TP services we work in your document management system, your templates and your naming conventions, with access limited to the engagement team. [Confirm your data-handling and access policy here.]

What do you need from us to start?

A signed NDA, the completed benchmarking request or documentation brief, prior-year documentation if any, the financials of the tested party and any group agreements relevant to the transaction.

Talk through a white-label arrangement.

Tell us the volume and the formats you work in. We will tell you what we can commit to, and what we cannot.
